What the trials changed

  • Per-size cost weights replace a single uniform multiplier. Early growth still requires eating; all objects keep their existing point values.
  • Earned growth fills at a bounded rate of 4.5 seconds per size, preventing a large harvest from skipping several sizes. Score is immediate; growth cannot advance beyond earned points. Current size pickups still advance instantly.
  • Food reserves are checked nearby, and new clusters fit within that search area. Placement avoids solid footprints and blocked direct approaches.
  • Obsolete miniature size boosts no longer skip late-game levels.
  • Expanded-city tiles reduced from eight to four. Landscape cells reduced from 121 to 49; the final map has four countries and six city objects instead of sixteen countries and twelve cities. Required growth still has a 150% food reserve.
  • Travel speed at larger map scales increased; uncapped extra-size steps are 4 units instead of 0.7. Autoplay can approach landscape food near the hole rim when a larger neighbor blocks a closer approach.

Per-size timing

The slowest final recorded interval is 9.80s, versus 92s in the baseline. Ordinary fully played sizes usually last about 4.5–8s. Rows marked * end at a map transition or victory and may be partial. A size can also be split across adjacent map rows; these short fragments are not skipped gameplay. Tier names identify the growth scale, not necessarily food available on that map.

Method and limits

Real Game.update with drawing disabled; final runs use 1/60-second steps. Each run starts with microbes and ends with World cleared, before optional Next city. Baseline used 1/30-second steps. Trials include the production movement, collisions, crowds, swallowing animations, boosts, food reserves and world transitions. These are autoplay gameplay-clock measurements, not human completion-time guarantees or frame-rate benchmarks. Manual route choice can take longer. Intervals split by a map transition can appear as two shorter rows for the same size; they should be considered together. Final victory can end a size early. A full browser run is recorded separately in pacing-browser-samples.json.

Reproduce with Node 22.12 or newer: npm run test:pacing. The command writes /tmp/city-pacing.json and fails for incomplete runs, totals outside 180–300 seconds or a recorded interval over 15 seconds. Use TRIAL_OUTPUT to choose another output path.
